01 — The Argument

For decades, luxury was sold by the square metre of the city.

Expensive marble. Views of other buildings. Scarcity, manufactured.

Scapeland starts somewhere else.

We believe real luxury is simple, and almost always outside — space, light, silence, horizon, and what they give back to the body: deeper sleep, slower mornings, food from the ground it grew in, heat and cold water, movement under the oaks. The things cities stopped offering and started charging extra for. Scapeland brings them back to the centre of the offer. Not as extras. As the product itself.

02 — To Live Well

Wellbeing is the masterplan.

A Scapeland village is drawn around the body as much as the land. Food that grows a short walk from the table, and a wellness pavilion built in the same timber and glass as the houses — sauna, ice bath, massage, studio, gym. Heat, cold, movement, stillness — designed in from the first sketch, not added on.

Shared spaces vary from village to village — the land decides the set. For owners, Kept takes care of the rest.

The setting

Outdoor is the address.

We choose the land before we draw the house — birch forest, open valley, the edge of the water. Space, light, silence, horizon. The view was here long before the walls.

The making

Built in timber. Built to last.

A mass-timber Core and a passive-house Skin — warm, quiet, low-carbon. Engineered to a standard the market kept for far more; the craft is in everything you never have to think about.

03 — The System

Solid.Proprietary construction system

Five years of refinement. One purpose.

To deliver premium architecture at a price the market said wasn't possible.

Solid is built on five technologies. Each one named, each one proprietary. Together, they make a house that goes up in weeks, lasts for decades, and costs what it should — not what the market got used to charging.

  • 01 Core The structural shellThe mass timber frame. The bones of every Scapeland house. Engineered for span, light, and a quiet that conventional construction can't match.
  • 02 Skin The envelopeInsulation and thermal performance certified to passive house standards. The layer that decides how the house breathes with the climate.
  • 03 Breath The climate systemMechanical ventilation, heating, cooling. Quiet enough that you forget it's there.
  • 04 Grid The energy infrastructureSolar, storage, energy management. Most Scapeland houses cover the majority of their annual consumption.
  • 05 Frame The modular logicPre-fabrication, assembly, delivery. How a Scapeland house gets built in weeks instead of years.
