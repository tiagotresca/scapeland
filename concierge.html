Scapeland Concierge.

You arrive. Everything else is done.

A personal assistant, a mobile app and a circle of curated locals — so that every stay at your Scapeland house begins the moment you close the car door, not two errands later. An escape from stress, designed end to end for recovery.

Before you arrive

The house is ready.

Send your grocery list from the app — or let the garden decide. The fridge is cold and full, the bread is from the village, the eggs are from the coop you can see from the deck.

The house is aired, the beds are made, the sauna is booked for the hour you land. You drive in; nothing is waiting for you except the evening.

While you're here

Wellbeing, on demand.

Mud therapy. A nutritionist who plans the week around the garden. A massage in the pavilion — or at your house. A pilates teacher at sunrise, a cold-water coach, a guided walk through the montado.

All local, all vetted, all one message away. The best practitioners of the region, kept close so your recovery doesn't depend on your planning.
